# Code of Conduct

TeamCraft follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1. This is a short reference; the full text there is the reference
for interpretation.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in TeamCraft a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests, reviews and chats.
- Assume good faith and give clear, specific feedback about the work, not the
  person.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes, apologise and learn from them.
- Keep private information of others, such as personal data or customer data
  from a deployment, out of issues and pull requests.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insults, personal or political attacks, and harassment in public or
  private.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (the repository, its issues, pull
requests and discussions) and when a person represents the project in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the project maintainers, by a
private GitHub security advisory on the repository, marked as a Code of Conduct
report. Do not post the details in a public issue. The maintainers review every
report and respond as soon as they can. Reports are handled with confidentiality
for the people involved.

Security problems are not Code of Conduct reports; follow
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for them.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may take the action that fits the case, in the order of the
Contributor Covenant: a correction, a warning, a temporary ban, or a permanent
ban from project spaces. Anyone who is the subject of an enforcement decision
may ask the maintainers for a review of it.
